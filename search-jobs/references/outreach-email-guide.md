# Outreach email — style guide

Read this before drafting each email.

## Principles (non-negotiable)
- **Language: English**, always.
- **Length: ~150-200 words max.** Short, dense, zero filler.
- **Voice: first person.** Professional, direct, warm, never obsequious.
- **First sentence = a concrete hook**: a specific fact that ties your
  profile to THIS posting (not "I am writing to apply for..."). Prove in
  one line that you've read the posting.
- **Prove you read the posting**: identify 2-3 key requirements from the
  description and match them precisely to real projects of yours.
- **One clear CTA** at the end (a short call / 15 minutes).
- **No filler phrases** ("I am a hard-working team player," "passionate
  about excellence"...). No exaggeration, nothing invented.

## Structure
1. **Hook (1-2 sentences)** — a concrete fact tying your experience to the posting.
2. **Body (2-4 sentences)** — 2-3 requirements from the posting <-> your
   projects (include metrics).
3. **Fit + motivation (1 sentence)** — why this company / this role, now.
4. **CTA (1 sentence)** — proposing a short call.
5. **Standard signature.**

## Recipient
- If a named contact exists: "Hi [First name],".
- Otherwise (default free pipeline, contacts left empty): address it
  generically, e.g. "Dear [Company] Hiring Team," / "Dear Hiring Manager,".

## Picking your hooks

Pull hooks from `context-input/Candidate-Profile.md`'s "key achievements"
section — each one there is tagged with the role types it's best suited
for. Match the posting's role type to the tag, and pick the 1-2 hooks that
map most directly onto its top requirements. Don't reuse the same hook for
every email; the whole point is that each email reads as if it was written
specifically for that posting.

## Standard signature

Build this once in `Candidate-Profile.md` and reuse it verbatim, e.g.:
```
Best regards,
[Your name]
[Your current title] — [Your current company] ([City])
[email] · [LinkedIn URL]
```

## Example structure (generic — replace every bracket)

> Dear [Company] Hiring Team,
>
> Your [Role Title] posting calls for [top requirement from the JD] —
> exactly what I did at [your company/project] when I [concrete action] and
> [concrete, quantified result].
>
> [1-2 more sentences connecting a second hook to a second JD requirement,
> with a metric.]
>
> [1 sentence: why this company/role specifically, tied to something real
> about them, not generic flattery.]
>
> Could we set up a quick 15-minute call to explore the fit?
>
> Best regards,
> [Your name]
> [Your current title] — [Your current company] ([City])
> [email] · [LinkedIn URL]
